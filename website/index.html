How it works

    Record

    Pick a screen or window, turn on camera, microphone or system audio, and press Record.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is it really free?

Yes. Recorder Studio is open source under the MIT license — no watermark, no subscription, no time limit.

Why does Windows show a SmartScreen warning?

The installer is not code-signed yet. Click “More info”, then “Run anyway”. The source code is public on GitHub.

Do I need a powerful GPU?

No. Any GPU with hardware H.264 (most Intel, NVIDIA and AMD from the last decade) makes stopping and exporting much faster; otherwise the CPU is used automatically.

Are my recordings uploaded anywhere?

Never. Recordings, projects and exports stay in Videos\Recorder Studio on your computer.

Can I change the camera layout after recording?

Yes. Screen and camera are separate tracks, so you can switch between overlay, split, full camera, cutout or screen only in the editor.
